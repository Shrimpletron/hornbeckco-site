Accessibility effective 2026-08-05

If any of this is hard to use, say so and it gets fixed.

Hornbeckco targets WCAG 2.2 AA on this site. Current measures: semantic HTML, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, system fonts, AA-or-better contrast throughout, no scripts, and layouts tested from 320px up.

Known limitations

The PDF documents (specimen packet, data-handling memo, engagement terms) are not yet tagged for assistive technology. Until they are, the memo's content is summarized in accessible HTML on the home page, and any document's content is available in an accessible format on request.

Feedback

If anything on this site or in the documents is difficult to access, email hello@hornbeckco.com and it will be corrected or provided in another format. One operator reads this inbox; accessibility requests get same-business-day acknowledgment.
